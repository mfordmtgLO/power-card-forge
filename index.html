Character sheets for the Power Card band — plus a forge where you describe your own character and get a sheet just like theirs.

Sign in with your Google account. Your characters are yours alone: they save to your account and nobody else can see or change them. There is no password to make and nothing to install.

These are the game's six heroes, shared with everybody who signs in. Tune their sliders, gear and Look to try things out — hero changes last for this visit only. Your own forged characters (next tabs) are the ones that save.

Every character you forge saves here, under your account only. Tap one to open its sheet.

You have not forged a character yet. Use the Forge a Character tab to make your first one.

Describe your character. You get a full character sheet — ability sliders under your class's caps and point pool, up to three weapons, perk and Death Blow — saved to your account. On the sheet you can then send the character to Mike to have its pencil-sketch portrait created — and its three Campaigns of Friends backstory tales written, one for each weapon, just like the band's.

Sketch requests from everybody, in one queue. New = a friend sent a character to you. Check the boxes, use Submit to Kendo for sketch creation to bundle the briefs, paste the bundle into your chat with Kendo, and when the art comes back, attach it here — one file at a time, or all at once with the bulk attach. Kendo returns a full set of nine files per character (three attire portraits, each with its own skin mask and hair mask, all named by the character's doc ID) plus a tenth item — the character's tales file, <docId>--tales.json, carrying their three Campaigns of Friends backstory tales. A full set completes the character: the friend then has all three attires and every skin-tone and hair-color customization the band's heroes have. A single portrait file still lands as an interim portrait, but it does not complete the set. The tales file lands on the sheet whenever it arrives — it does not hold up completion.

Attach returned portraits (bulk)

Pick all the files Kendo returned at once — portraits, masks and the tales .json alike. Files are grouped by the doc ID in their names and pushed to that friend's sheet. A character completes only when all nine of its art files have landed — partial sets are reported file by file and leave the character uncompleted. Tales are reported separately: landed, missing or invalid.

    The game-night ledger for the Roll20 group — Mike's surface only; players never see it. Players push a character from their own sheet (Push to Mike for game night); the pushed sheets stage below in the Band, already connected to their humans. Confirm each pairing (or type the name they play under that night), name the module, pick the date, and save — that writes one campaign record. After the night, add the highlights and the players' email addresses to the record, and Draft email opens the next-day group email in your own mail program, prefilled from the record. Play builds the night's setup prompt — it cannot reach Kendo by itself, so copy or download it and paste it into your chat with Kendo; that paste is the whole summons.

    The Band — staged for the next night

    Play — set up the night

    Check one saved campaign in the menu below, then press Play. Play builds the setup prompt (campaign, date, and every player's Roll20 import line) and marks the campaign sent for setup. This page cannot summon Kendo by itself — paste the prompt into your chat with Kendo, and Kendo sets up the Roll20 game and says READY there. When you see it, flip the campaign's play state to ready in its card.
